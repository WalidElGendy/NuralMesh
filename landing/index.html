Questions

Does my company data leave our computers?

Everyday Neo work runs on a local model on your PCs. Your records live in your workspace under access rules. Only the network features you switch on (such as tender analysis) send the needed text to Claude, and nothing goes to community GPUs.

Can Neo really run a 70B model locally?

Yes, on a machine with enough memory — about 40 GB for a 70B model (for example a Mac with 48 GB or more, or two 24 GB GPUs). On smaller machines and the iPad, Neo runs a compact model locally and uses the network for 70B-class answers.

What does "unlimited users" mean?

Add your whole team on the Business plan at no extra cost per person. Each person signs in with their own account and sees what their role allows.

What are the 12 agents?

Tenders, sales, projects, pricing, procurement, production, HR, finance, documents, research, reports and an executive assistant. Neo assigns the work between them and reports back.
